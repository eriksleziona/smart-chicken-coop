# Maintenance plan

## 1. Routine inspection

- check fan intake and exhaust for dust and debris
- inspect sensor housings and connectors for dust and humidity
- verify door mechanism travel and limits
- check water flow and feed-level sensor condition
- confirm enclosure seal and condensation status

## 2. Planned replacement

- filter media and dust protection if used
- sensor replacement based on drift or failure history
- actuator and mechanical wear review
- replace fuses if required by service event and inspect root cause

## 3. Service records

Keep a maintenance log with:

- date
- operating condition
- observed anomaly
- repair or recalibration
- responsible person

## 4. Design priority

The design should allow routine maintenance without opening the mains compartment when the low-voltage zone can be serviced separately.
